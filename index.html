1. Summary

Myxel is a fully offline personal journaling application. It is designed so that everything you create stays entirely on your own device. We want to be direct and unambiguous:

  • We do not collect any personal data.
  • We do not transmit any of your data off your device.
  • We have no servers, accounts, cloud services, or internet connection of any kind in the App.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party data-collection tools.
  • We have no access to your content whatsoever, and no technical means to retrieve, view, or recover it.

The only data-related interactions that can occur are those you deliberately initiate (such as adding a photo you select, or exporting a backup file you choose to save or share), and those handled entirely by third parties outside our control (such as the app store you downloaded from, or your device's operating system) — described in Sections 6 and 7.

2. Information You Create and Store

When you use Myxel, you may create content such as text entries ("moments"), captions, moods, tags, places you manually type, dates, and photographs you choose to add ("Your Content"). Your Content is:

  • Stored exclusively in local, application-private storage on your own device.
  • Encrypted at rest for text and metadata, using an encryption key held in your device's secure hardware-backed keystore.
  • Never uploaded, synced, transmitted, or backed up to any cloud service by the App, or otherwise sent off your device by Myxel.

Because Your Content never leaves your device and we have no servers, the Developer cannot see, access, collect, retrieve, restore, or recover Your Content under any circumstances.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

For the avoidance of doubt, Myxel does not collect, request, generate, or process any of the following: your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials (there are no accounts); your location or GPS data (the App requests no location permission); your contacts, calendar, microphone, or camera; advertising identifiers, device identifiers, or cookies; usage analytics, crash reports, diagnostics, or telemetry; or any behavioral, marketing, or profiling data. The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking software development kits (SDKs).

4. Photographs and Device Permissions

If you choose to add photographs to a moment, the App uses your device's operating-system photo picker to let you select images. When you add a photo:

  • A copy of the selected photo is stored in the App's private storage on your device.
  • The App removes embedded location (GPS) and other sensitive metadata (EXIF) from photos you add going forward, before storing the copy. However, you are solely responsible for the content of the photos you choose to add.
  • The App does not request or use location services, the camera permission, background location, contacts, microphone, or network/internet permissions.

Any permission the App does request is used solely on-device for the feature you invoked, and never to collect or transmit information to us or anyone else.

5. No Internet Access

Myxel is built without internet permission. The App itself has no capability to connect to the internet, and therefore cannot and does not transmit, upload, share, or leak Your Content or any usage information to us or to any third party. The only exception is that, if you tap a link we provide (such as the link to this Privacy Policy), your device's own web browser — a separate application governed by its own terms — will open to display that page; the App does not itself fetch or transmit any data in doing so.

6. Third Parties and Platforms We Do Not Control

While Myxel itself collects and transmits nothing, you should understand that the environment in which any app runs involves third parties whose data practices are entirely outside the Developer's control and are governed by their policies, not this one. These may include, without limitation:

  • App stores (Google Play, Apple App Store). When you download, install, update, purchase, or manage the App, the store operator (Google LLC or Apple Inc.) may collect information such as your account details, device identifiers, download and purchase records, and diagnostics, in accordance with its own privacy policy. This happens between you and the store; the App neither performs nor receives any of it.
  • Your device operating system (Android, iOS). Your device's operating system and its manufacturer may process information about app usage, storage, permissions, and system-level backups according to their own settings and policies. For example, a device-level or cloud backup feature you have enabled at the operating-system or account level (such as an Android or iCloud device backup) is controlled by you and your platform, not by the App. Myxel sets standard flags to opt out of automatic system backup of its data where the platform supports this, but you remain responsible for your own device and account backup settings.
  • Any app or service you choose to interact with. If you use another application (for example, a file manager, a messaging app, an email app such as Gmail, or a cloud-storage service) to save, open, move, or share a backup file you exported from Myxel, that other application or service is a third party governed by its own terms and privacy policy. Once data leaves Myxel through your action, its handling is your responsibility and that of the service you chose.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of your app store, device manufacturer, operating system, and any third-party app or service you use in connection with Myxel. The Developer is not responsible for, and this policy does not cover, the data practices of any such third party.

7. Backups You Create Yourself

Myxel allows you to manually export a backup file of your data, and to restore from such a file. Please understand the following, as you are entirely responsible for any backup file you create and for what you do with it:

  • A backup is only ever created when you explicitly choose to export one. The App never creates or transmits backups automatically.
  • When you export a backup, you choose where it is saved or whether it is shared (for example, saving it to your device's storage, or sharing it through another app you select).
  • Backup files are encrypted with a password that you set. You are solely responsible for choosing a strong password and for keeping that password safe. If you lose or forget the password, the backup cannot be decrypted or recovered by anyone, including the Developer.
  • Once a backup file leaves the App — for example, if you save it to a shared folder, send it to another person, attach it to an email, upload it to a cloud service, or move it to another device — it is entirely outside the App's control and outside the scope of this policy. Its security and privacy become your responsibility and are governed by the terms of whatever storage, service, or party you transfer it to.
  • The Developer has no access to, copy of, or knowledge of any backup file you create.

8. Your Responsibilities

Because Myxel stores everything locally and privately on your device, the security of Your Content depends substantially on how you secure and use your own device. You are responsible for:

  • Securing your device with a device-level lock (such as a PIN, password, pattern, or biometric lock). Myxel does not provide its own in-app lock, so anyone with access to your unlocked device may be able to open the App and view Your Content.
  • Any photos, text, or other content you choose to create, add, or store in the App, and ensuring you have the right to use such content and that it complies with applicable law.
  • Managing, storing, and protecting any backup files you export, and any passwords you set for them.
  • Any data loss that may result from, among other things, deleting the App, deleting content within the App (which is permanent and cannot be undone), resetting or losing your device, device failure, operating-system changes, or losing a backup password.
  • Your own device, account, operating-system, and third-party app settings, including any system-level or cloud backup features you have enabled.

9. Data Deletion and Retention

We do not retain any of your data, because we never receive it. Your Content remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Deleting a moment permanently removes it and its associated photo files from your device with no undo. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all of its locally stored data. Because we have no servers and no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our end and no way for us to restore anything after it is deleted from your device. Any data collected by a third party (such as an app store) is subject to that third party's own retention and deletion practices, which you must pursue with them directly.

10. Children's Privacy

Myxel does not collect any data from anyone, including children. The App is a general-audience, offline journaling tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction, such as 16 in parts of the European Union). Because we collect no information whatsoever, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the App is not designed to be attractive to or targeted at children. If you are a parent or guardian and permit a child to use the App on a device, you are responsible for supervising that use. If you believe a child has provided information to us — which, given the App's design, is not technically possible — please contact us so we can address your concern.

11. Security

We implement reasonable technical measures within the App to protect Your Content on your device, including at-rest encryption of text and metadata using a key held in your device's secure keystore, isolation of App data in private application storage, removal of sensitive metadata from newly added photos, opting out of automatic system backup where the platform supports it, and preventing the App's contents from appearing in the device's recent-apps preview or screenshots. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, particularly if your device is lost, stolen, compromised, "rooted," "jailbroken," running modified software, or accessed by someone to whom you have granted access. You acknowledge that you provide and store Your Content at your own risk.

12. International Users

Myxel processes no data on any server and transfers no data across borders, because all processing happens locally on your own device. Regardless of where you are located, Your Content stays on your device. Where data-protection laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) apply, please note that because we do not collect, process, sell, or share any personal data, the rights those laws grant (such as access, correction, deletion, or opt-out of sale) are satisfied inherently: you already hold, control, and can delete all of your own data directly on your device at any time, and there is no data held by us to request or object to.
