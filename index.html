Privacy Policy for Mommento

Effective date: July 30, 2026  •  Last updated: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Mommento ("Mommento," "the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles information. Mommento is developed and maintained by Talheh Ahrari ("the Developer"). This policy applies to your use of the App on any device on which you install it.

By downloading, installing, or using Mommento, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, you must not install or use the App.

1. Summary

Mommento is a fully offline personal journaling application. It is designed so that everything you create stays entirely on your own device. We want to be direct and unambiguous:

  • We do not collect any personal data.
  • We do not transmit any of your data off your device.
  • We do not have any servers, accounts, or cloud services. The App has no ability to send your content anywhere.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party data-collection tools of any kind.
  • We have no access to your content whatsoever, and no technical means to retrieve, view, or recover it.

2. Information You Create and Store

When you use Mommento, you may create content such as text entries ("moments"), captions, moods, tags, places you manually type, dates, and photographs you choose to add ("Your Content"). Your Content is:

  • Stored exclusively in local, application-private storage on your own device.
  • Encrypted at rest for text and metadata, using an encryption key stored in your device's secure hardware-backed keystore.
  • Never uploaded, synced, transmitted, backed up to any cloud service by the App, or otherwise sent off your device by Mommento.

Because Your Content never leaves your device and we have no servers, the Developer cannot see, access, collect, retrieve, restore, or recover Your Content under any circumstances.

3. Photographs and Device Permissions

If you choose to add photographs to a moment, the App uses your device's operating-system photo picker to let you select images. When you add a photo:

  • A copy of the selected photo is stored in the App's private storage on your device.
  • The App removes embedded location (GPS) and other sensitive metadata (EXIF) from photos you add going forward, before storing the copy. However, you are solely responsible for the content of the photos you choose to add.
  • The App does not request or use location services, the camera permission, background location, contacts, microphone, or network/internet permissions.

4. No Internet Access

Mommento is built without internet permission. The App has no capability to connect to the internet, and therefore cannot and does not transmit, upload, share, or leak Your Content or any usage information to us or to any third party.

5. Backups You Create Yourself

Mommento allows you to manually export a backup file (a .mmbak file) of your data, and to restore from such a file. Please understand the following, as you are entirely responsible for any backup file you create and for what you do with it:

  • A backup is only ever created when you explicitly choose to export one. The App never creates or transmits backups automatically.
  • When you export a backup, you choose where it is saved or whether it is shared (for example, saving it to your device's storage or sharing it through another app you select).
  • Backup files are encrypted with a password that you set. You are solely responsible for choosing a strong password and for keeping that password safe. If you lose or forget the password, the backup cannot be decrypted or recovered by anyone, including the Developer.
  • Once a backup file leaves the App — for example, if you save it to a shared folder, send it to another person, upload it to a cloud service, or move it to another device — it is entirely outside the App's control and outside the scope of this policy. The security and privacy of that file becomes your responsibility and is governed by the terms of whatever storage, service, or party you transfer it to.
  • The Developer has no access to, copy of, or knowledge of any backup file you create.

6. Your Responsibilities

Because Mommento stores everything locally and privately on your device, the security of Your Content depends substantially on how you secure and use your own device. You are responsible for:

  • Securing your device with a device-level lock (such as a PIN, password, pattern, or biometric lock). Mommento does not provide its own in-app lock, so anyone with access to your unlocked device may be able to open the App and view Your Content.
  • Any photos, text, or other content you choose to create, add, or store in the App, and ensuring you have the right to use such content.
  • Managing, storing, and protecting any backup files you export, and any passwords you set for them.
  • Any data loss that may result from, among other things, deleting the App, deleting content within the App (which is permanent and cannot be undone), resetting or losing your device, device failure, operating-system changes, or losing a backup password.
  • Complying with all applicable laws in your use of the App and Your Content.

7. Data Deletion and Retention

We do not retain any of your data, because we never receive it. Your Content remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Deleting a moment permanently removes it and its associated photo files from your device with no undo. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all of its locally stored data. Because we have no servers and no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our end and no way for us to restore anything after it is deleted from your device.

8. Children's Privacy

Mommento does not collect any data from anyone, including children. The App is a general-audience, offline journaling tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Because we collect no information whatsoever, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and permit a child to use the App on a device, you are responsible for supervising that use.

9. Third-Party Services

The App itself integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services. Note that if you obtained Mommento through an app distribution platform (such as the Google Play Store), that platform has its own privacy policy and terms governing your download and use of apps through it, which are outside our control. Similarly, if you use another app or service of your choosing to store or share a backup file you exported, that app or service is a third party governed by its own terms, not by this policy.

10. Security

We implement reasonable technical measures within the App to protect Your Content on your device, including at-rest encryption of text and metadata using a key held in your device's secure keystore, isolation of App data in private application storage, removal of sensitive metadata from newly added photos, and preventing the App's contents from appearing in the device's recent-apps preview or screenshots. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, particularly if your device is lost, stolen, compromised, "rooted," "jailbroken," running modified software, or accessed by someone to whom you have granted access. You acknowledge that you provide and store Your Content at your own risk.
